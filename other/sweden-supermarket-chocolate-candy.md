# Chocolate & Candy Section at a Large Swedish Supermarket 🇸🇪
This video shows how large the chocolate & candy section can be in an extremely well-developed market: Sweden.

There is also a pick & mix section of similar size not shown in the video (briefly glanced in the background at 1m32s). We will shoot this as well next time.

The video is shot at a large store format supermarket in Scania province. There are many supermarkets of equal size in the country.

The video is intended to be informational, aspirational and inspirational for executives in countries with less developed chocolate & candy categories.
