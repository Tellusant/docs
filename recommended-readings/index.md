# External Recommended Readings
Here we recommend high quality readings by external authorities, touching on our mission on to transform strategy development. ***These are things we wish we had written.***

---
#### [A. Banerjee and E. Duflo: *The Economic Lives of the Poor*](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/jep.21.1.141)
This groundbreaking easy-to-read paper on the lives of poor people around the world was a main contributor to their Nobel Prize in Economics award. It has influnced our quantification of socioeconomic levels.

#### [A. Al-Haschimi et al. (2025): *Why Competition with China is Getting Tougher than Ever*. ECB](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/blog/date/2024/html/ecb.blog240903~57f1b63192.en.html)
This comparative paper between China and the US is amazingly fact based. Much to be learned from their approach beyond the interesting topic.

#### [Stata: *Analyzing Federal Reserve Posture with Stata Structural Break Tests*](https://www.stata.com/manuals14/tsestatsbsingle.pdf)
This is for statistics experts (PhD required). It is an insightful analysis of how the Federal Reserve switches between full employment and low inflation postures, its two goals.
