#### [ECB: Why competition with China is getting tougher than ever (Sep. 2025)](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/blog/date/2024/html/ecb.blog240903~57f1b63192.en.html)
by Alexander Al-Haschimi, Lorenz Emter, Vanessa Gunnella, Iván Ordoñez Martínez, Tobias Schuler and Tajda Spital
#### [Analyzing Federal Reserve Posture with Stata Structural Break Tests](https://www.stata.com/manuals14/tsestatsbsingle.pdf)
This is for statistics experts (PhD required). It is an insightful analysis of how the Federal Reserve switches between full employment and low inflation postures, its two goals.
