

# Tellusant Etymology

What is the etymology of Tellusant? It derives from ***Tellus*** and ***Quantitative***.

***Tellus*** means Earth in Latin. We chose it because of the often global scope of our work for multinational companies.

***Quant*** because when we codify strategies on the platform, quantitative information / knowledge is central to the planning process. Quantification through statistical methods and AI is the heart of our company. Translating the numbers into meaningful messages for the CEO and the C-suite is our soul.

As secondary *-ant* attributes, we thought of *Relevant* and *Elegant*.

*Relevant* because we build solutions that are relevant to management users, rather than us having a "product out" mindset.

*Elegant* because the user interface has to be simple and appealing.

Not intended was the direct link to proto-indo-european *telh₂- which means "to endure",
which is central to any startup.  

Nor was the connection to Telluride, Colorado intended. But we might as well hold our first client conference there.
