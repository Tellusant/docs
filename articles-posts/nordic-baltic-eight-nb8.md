# The Emerging Significance of the Nordic-Baltic Eight
The Nordic-Baltic Eight (NB8) is a regional cooperation forum of increasing importance. The total population is around 35 million and given the high standard of living, it ranks fourth in the Single Market (EU + 4) by GDP.

It is therefore an important market for any company that wants to succeed in Europe. This is compounded by the fact that it is the most inventive region in Europe, as well as people having a propensity to try new products and services. A European California in some sense. So non-NB8 CEOs and management teams, take notice when you develop strategies.
