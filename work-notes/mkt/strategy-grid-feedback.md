# Review of *The Strategy Grid* SSRN Preprint with Feedback

## Overall assessment

The core idea is strong and well suited to a short SSRN preprint. The
main work is not expansion but reframing: more neutral tone, clearer
theoretical positioning, and a more conventional academic structure.

## Major recommendations

### 1. Academic opening

-   Add an abstract (120--180 words)
-   Add keywords
-   Add JEL classifications
-   Begin with the paper's contribution and scope.

### 2. Clarify the primitives

Three economic primitives: - Growth - Productivity - Risk

applied across: - Macro - Company - Business Unit

creating nine analytical cells.

### 3. Rename 'Proof of Strategy Grid'

Prefer: - Theoretical Foundation - Economic Derivation - Derivation from
Firm-Value Theory

### 4. Moderate promotional language

Replace categorical statements with qualified academic language.

### 5. Remove product promotion

Move references to commercial products to an implementation or
disclosure note.

### 6. Clarify what the grid is

Describe it as a diagnostic framework that organizes the economic
variables strategy ultimately seeks to influence.

### 7. Explain how managers use it

Explain how priorities emerge from benchmarking, gap analysis,
materiality, and managerial influence.

## Technical comments

### Productivity

-   Use Total Factor Productivity consistently.
-   Treat structural productivity as your interpretation.
-   Cite Solow and productivity literature.
-   Clarify α.

### Risk

-   Distinguish risk from volatility.
-   Specify variables, frequency, horizon, and downside treatment.

### Growth

Clarify: - historical vs expected growth - nominal vs real growth

## Illustrations

Retain them, but add: - figure numbers - titles - sources -
methodology - sample definitions - units - time period

## Suggested structure

1.  Abstract
2.  Introduction
3.  Theoretical Foundation
4.  The Strategy Grid
5.  Operationalization
6.  Illustrations
7.  Discussion
8.  Conclusion
9.  References
10. Author note

## Title recommendation

**The Strategy Grid: A Diagnostic Framework for Growth, Productivity,
and Risk**

## Suggested JEL codes

-   D24
-   G32
-   L25
-   M10

Optional: - O47

## Overall conclusion

The paper already contains the right ideas. The remaining work is
primarily editorial: - strengthen academic framing - reduce promotional
tone - sharpen definitions - add references - clarify theoretical
positioning

Keep it concise. Its strength is its parsimony.
